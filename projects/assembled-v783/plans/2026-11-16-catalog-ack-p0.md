## Read-only discovery, not yet a demonstrated customer incident
In the assembled browser `app/docs/mock-backend.js`, `_fetchInterno` returns HTTP responses from mutations and its `finally` invokes `guardarEstadoLocal()` without awaiting its possible Promise. The existing `POST /api/ubicaciones` returns `J(nueva)` before that `finally`. The non-strict save can fail or reject under storage errors or cross-tab conflicts. Thus HTTP 2xx does **not**, on inspection, prove durable save. The WebKit tab test independently checks cold-read after 2xx and failed in the prior full audit once; causality is NOT established. This is a precise hypothesis worth RED instrumentation, not permission to overwrite a shop.
